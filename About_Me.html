Hello, my name is Dzhaner. My passion for technology started at a young age. I'll divide it into stages:

Stage 1: Kindergarten

I used to have a used PS1, which I couldn't play for long as it broke. However, I remember wondering how it worked. You would put a little card in it, and each card had a different game. After it broke, my parents abandoned it, but I remained curious about it for months. Eventually, I decided to open it up, along with the memory card. It was so interesting to me, even though I didn't know what any of the parts were.

Stage 2: Primary School

This is when I got my first phone and started to learn a little about technology. This is also when I discovered my first bug, which was in my service provider's system. The way it worked was that you could start mobile data and then remove your SIM card. You couldn't open websites, but if you hosted a server, the server theoretically used your mobile data without charging you. This meant you could browse freely through the cloud. I discovered it unintentionally while trying to play Minecraft Pocket Edition with my friends. None of us had mobile data to host a server, so I started experimenting and found this bug. By the end of primary school, I had saved enough money to buy my own computer parts, built a computer, and used a friend's computer to install Windows on a flash drive and boot it up.

Stage 3: Secondary School

At the start of secondary school, we had to pick an after-class activity, so I chose web development. Unfortunately, it was only available for the first year. Later, I became interested in Linux but wanted a separate machine for it. Since that wasn't a necessity, I wanted to make the money on my own. My friends knew I was good with technology, so one asked me to download Windows on his laptop. After that, another friend wanted me to open his laptop and clean it. Soon, word spread, and I started changing small parts like network interface controllers, RAM, and keyboards. I also began selling computer parts, which I salvaged from a store 70 km from my town. This store sold computers with mostly good parts that didn't turn on, and the prices were extremely discounted.

Stage 4: Present Time

I am now using my money to get certifications and work on projects, which I sometimes record on this portfolio.
